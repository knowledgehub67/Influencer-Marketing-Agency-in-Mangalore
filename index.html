Understanding Influencer Marketing

Influencer marketing is a form of digital marketing in which a brand works with a content creator to communicate with the creator's audience. The collaboration can involve videos, photographs, stories, reviews, event coverage or other agreed content formats.

The objective is not simply to find a creator with a large following. Audience relevance, content quality, platform, location, engagement and campaign goals can all be considered when selecting creators. Businesses researching an Influencer Marketing Agency in Mangalore can use these factors when evaluating a campaign approach.

How Influencer Campaigns Work

01

Set the Campaign Goal

Define whether the campaign is intended to support awareness, engagement, product communication, event promotion or another measurable marketing objective.

02

Choose Relevant Creators

Creator selection can consider audience location, content category, platform, audience fit, engagement and the type of content required.

03

Plan the Content

Campaign briefs can define the message, format, publishing schedule, required disclosures and other agreed deliverables.

04

Launch the Collaboration

The creator publishes the agreed content while campaign activity and communication are managed according to the campaign plan.

05

Measure Performance

Campaign reporting can include available measures such as reach, views, engagement, clicks or other agreed performance indicators.

06

Review and Improve

Performance information can be used to understand what content, platforms and creator types were relevant to the campaign.
